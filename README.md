# IMovies-Mini-Project-
IMovies is a modern MERN stack movie platform featuring mood recommendations, transparent pricing, 10-minute seat holds, Razorpay payments, and QR e-tickets. Styled in dark/red, it eliminates booking friction and hidden fees 

✨ Features🍿 

1. Smart Discovery & Mood RecommendationsMood-Based Engine:
   Filter movies by your current vibe (Thrill-Seeking, Feel-Good, Action, Drama) or use the "Surprise Me" pick to solve decision fatigue.
   Regional Filters: Filter seamlessly across multiple regional languages and audio/subtitle configurations.
   Integrated Trailers & Ratings: View combined critic and user ratings along with official trailers and cast details.

2. Transparent Booking & Interactive SeatsInteractive Seat Matrix:
   Real-time seat selection with an automatic 10-minute temporary seat hold to prevent double bookings.
   Zero Hidden Fees: What you see is what you pay—upfront transparent pricing with no surprises at checkout.
   Group Split Payment: Easily calculate cost breakdown for group movie outings.

3. Secure Payments & E-TicketsRazorpay Integration:
   Simulated secure payment processing supporting UPI, credit/debit cards, and digital wallets.
   QR-Coded E-Tickets: Instant e-ticket generation with scannable QR codes stored directly in your profile/watchlist.
   Auto-Refund Safeguard: Built-in automatic transaction recovery and refund processing for failed attempts.
